About the ATNP Database

ATNP is a curated resource for plant-derived anticancer natural products. It integrates compound, plant, taxonomic, and MS/MS spectral information with tools for searching, browsing, spectral comparison, and evidence-aware interpretation.

Plant–compound associations are supported by spectral-entropy similarity matching and cross-referenced with published literature, NPBS records, and plant taxonomy. The Evidence Level shown on compound–plant pages follows an exclusive priority: A > B > C > D. Each association is assigned to the highest applicable level.

Evidence Levels
ADirect external support

The compound–plant species association has an exact species-level match in NPBS or published literature, regardless of analytical platform.

BPhylogenetic support

After excluding Level A, the association has NPBS/literature family- or genus-level support, or confirmed repeat support within the same platform.

CSame-platform match

After excluding Levels A and B, the association is supported by spectral matching between plant samples and reference spectra acquired on the same analytical platform.

DResidual associations

All remaining associations after Levels A, B, and C are assigned to Level D. This residual category keeps the four levels mutually exhaustive.
